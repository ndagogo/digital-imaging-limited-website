WHO WE ARE

Diagnostics with
a human touch.

Digital Imaging and Diagnostic Center is a diagnostic facility located on Okigwe Road, Owerri, serving individuals and families seeking medical laboratory and diagnostic imaging services.

We believe that the experience around a diagnostic investigation matters. From your first enquiry to your visit, our aim is to make information accessible, treat every person with respect and help patients understand the next steps in their care.

For details about a particular test, scan, appointment or preparation requirement, please contact our team directly. We will be glad to guide you.
